# Map data

`world_countries.geojson` and `world_countries_50m.geojson` contain Natural Earth
country boundaries at 110m and 50m scales. `world_admin1_regions.json.gz` contains
the first-level administrative boundaries (states, provinces, and comparable
regions) from Natural Earth 10m Admin-1, simplified for the game's map projection.
The source is Natural Earth 5.1.1:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/
Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

The map uses the original geographic outlines and region borders, with game
colors and interface styling. No game artwork is included. Regions without
Admin-1 coverage remain selectable as whole country territories.
The 1936 scenario groups present-day territories into period-era states and
colonial empires; its coastlines and small regional borders are an approximation,
not a survey-grade reconstruction of every 1936 boundary.
